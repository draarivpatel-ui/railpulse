Preloaded corridors use synthetic, regular-interval timetables for offline analysis. Headway, dwell and delay figures below are illustrative. Upload a real GTFS static feed to replace them with observed schedules.

Corridor map

Station positions are approximate, interpolated along published route geometry.

Color encodes mean delay at each station (blue: early or on time, red: late). Marker size is proportional to planned dwell.

Headway distribution

Observed gaps between consecutive trains as a multiple of the planned headway.

Headway by time of day

Mean and 90th-percentile headway per hour, against the planned headway.

Stringline (Marey) diagram

Each line is one train. Time runs left to right, distance bottom to top. Lines that converge or cross reveal bunching.

Dashed gray lines are the scheduled paths; colored lines are the observed or simulated trains.

Dwell by station

Mean observed dwell vs planned dwell, largest gaps first.

Delay along the line

Mean arrival delay vs schedule at each station, in route order.

Bunching and gap events

The most severe short headways (bunches) and long headways (gaps) observed across all stops.
