Marey graphic timetable (time-distance stringline)

Horizontal rules mark stations by cumulative route milepost. Sloped traces follow individual trains across the service day. Converging trajectories indicate vehicle bunching and overtakes.

Dashed charcoal traces represent scheduled paths; solid colored traces represent observed or simulated runs encoded by terminal arrival delay. Station codes and mileposts are indexed directly on the vertical axes.

Corridor alignment and station delay

Station coordinates interpolated along published route geometry.

Station marker color encodes mean arrival delay (blue: early or on schedule, red: delayed). Marker radius scales with scheduled station dwell.

Headway ratio distribution

Observed inter-train gaps normalized by scheduled headway (bunching ≤ 0.5x, gap ≥ 1.5x).

Hourly headway profile

Mean and 90th-percentile headway by hour of day compared against scheduled headway.

Station dwell comparison

Mean observed platform dwell versus scheduled stop duration, ranked by overrun.

Delay progression along route

Mean arrival offset from schedule at each station ordered by cumulative milepost.

Most severe compressed headways (bunches) and extended headways (gaps) recorded across all stations.

Preloaded corridors use regular-interval synthetic timetables over published station mileposts for offline inspection. Upload any GTFS static archive (.zip or CSV) to compute schedules and reliability metrics from agency feed files.
